# Maps and Tesla mods worth trying

Honest note: this comes from web search results only. Nobody has installed or driven any of it yet, and the sites that repost mods (modland, modshost and so on) are not vetted by BeamNG. Get mods from beamng.com/resources or the in-game repository when you can. FSD needs AI road data (the bridge reads the map's road graph), so a pretty map with no AI paths will not work with FSD.

## Maps (best first for FSD)
1. **East Coast USA (stock)**: residential and commercial mix, signals, stop signs. Already what the bridge is tested against, so this is the safe default.
2. **West Coast USA, Italy, Utah (stock)**: real AI roads, towns and highways. Italy has small towns with tight streets.
3. **[Los Injurus](https://www.beamng.com/resources/city-of-los-injurus.10021/)** (mod, beta): big city around a lake with expressways, subway and an abandoned mall, per its page. Very heavy: about 4 GB and it wants a 12 GB GPU for the high textures. Check on a laptop first. Unknown whether its roads have signals the bridge understands.
4. **[Band1t Highway](https://www.beamng.com/resources/band1t-highway.35612/)** (mod, beta): light map built for traffic. Highway more than city.
5. More lists: [City maps thread](https://www.beamng.com/threads/city-maps.94822/), [Terrains, Levels, Maps](https://www.beamng.com/resources/categories/terrains-levels-maps.9/), [AI traffic and FPS friendly maps](https://www.beamng.com/threads/ai-traffic-and-fps-friendly-maps.91738/).

I could not confirm any dedicated "residential plus commercial" mod by name beyond these, so if you want one, send me a beamng.com link and I will check what the bridge sees on it.

## Tesla mods
- **[Tesla Cybertruck](https://www.beamng.com/threads/tesla-cybertruck.66805/)** (beamng.com thread): tri-motor AWD EV, opening doors and frunk, 3 configs.
- **Tesla Pack** (Model S, Model 3 in two generations, Cybertruck; 9 mods listed) and **Tesla Model 3 pack** (21 configs, digital dash, drive modes): found on repost sites ([modland Tesla pack](https://www.modland.net/beamng.drive-mods/cars/tesla-pack-beamng.html), [Model 3 pack](https://www.modland.net/beamng.drive-mods/cars/tesla-model-3-pack.html)). Look for the original on beamng.com first.
- **Model X**: already in this repo, models/beamng/tesla-model-x.zip (issue #10 is making its doors open).

Cowork: install one at a time in mods\unpacked, check the console, then test the /test page. Do not install more than the user asked for.
